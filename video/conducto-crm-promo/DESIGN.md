# Conducto CRM — Design Reference

## Overview

Conducto CRM is a light, premium insurance-operations interface. The visual language combines a near-white canvas, royal-blue accents, dark ink typography, and translucent glass cards with generous rounded corners. Playfair Display gives the brand a confident editorial voice while Inter keeps the product UI crisp and practical. Blue gradients, soft borders, and diffused shadows create a calm sense of control.

## Colors

- **Canvas**: `#FDFDFF` — near-white page background.
- **Ink**: `#090B0F` — primary headings and high-contrast text.
- **Royal Blue**: `#0100FF` — primary actions and focus moments.
- **Brand Blue**: `#0014FF` — glass borders, icon treatments, and glow.
- **Electric Blue**: `#2166FF` — secondary highlights and data emphasis.
- **Muted Ink**: `#52555B` — subtitles, labels, and supporting copy.
- **Warning Orange**: `#F37513` — payment urgency and attention states.
- **Success Green**: `#2EA957` — paid or completed states.
- **Quiet Border**: `#D2D8E2` — inputs and low-emphasis dividers.

## Typography

- **Display**: Playfair Display, bold/700. Brand name, scene headlines, and hero statements.
- **UI**: Inter, 400–700. Body copy, navigation, data labels, and buttons.
- **Hierarchy**: oversized editorial statements (64–96px), section titles (32–48px), UI labels (20–24px), supporting copy (20px minimum for video legibility).

## Elevation

Use translucent white cards with a subtle royal-blue border and diffuse shadow: `rgba(0, 20, 255, 0.12) 0 8px 32px`. Depth comes from glass, blur, and localized blue radial glows rather than heavy black shadows. Rounded corners are generous: 16–24px.

## Components

- **Glass Dashboard Rail**: a vertical navigation panel with the CONDUCTO CRM wordmark and compact module labels.
- **Metric Tiles**: four rounded KPI cards with serif numbers, icon chips, and a small trend indicator.
- **Renewal Queue**: stacked policy rows with client, insurer, status, and due-date metadata.
- **Cobranza Signal**: a compact progress card that turns premium collection into a visible blue-to-green bar.
- **Quick Action Button**: royal-blue pill/rounded control with a right-arrow cue.
- **Captured Login Panel**: two-panel glass login layout from the source site, used as the opening brand frame.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the canvas light and spacious.
- Use royal blue as a precise signal, not a full-frame wash.
- Pair Playfair Display headlines with Inter product detail.
- Use glass cards, rounded corners, and soft blue shadows.
- Let data animate with clear, legible motion.

### Don'ts

- Do not switch to a dark or neon-first palette.
- Do not use generic dashboard colors unrelated to the captured system.
- Do not crowd cards or shrink labels below video-readable sizes.
- Do not use hard black drop shadows or sharp square containers.
